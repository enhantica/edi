## Scale and time-of-flight calibration

CeO₂ is the calibration standard, so its cell stays fixed and the DIFC terms put the peaks in place.

Filter the **Linked structures** and **Instrument** parameters, one category at a time, using the category filter, click the **Free** column header and choose **Free · all**, then press **Fit** in the status bar.
