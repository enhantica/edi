# Adaptive refinement of an actual Edi project

Version: 1.4.12. This is scientific guidance for actually offered operations. The agent chooses the steps and stopping point from evidence and the user's goal; no finite staged recipe or pass count is mandatory.

Inspect actual shape, units, weighting, exclusions, structure, calibration, variables, constraints and diagnostics; choose the next group from the dominant CURRENT residual. Preserve provenance and compare like objectives on comparable observations. A lower residual, save or absence of exposed errors does not establish physical validity without relevant evidence. Distinguish unavailable actions, unidentifiable variables and observed defects. Unavailable general diagnostics are unknown, not clean physical validation.

Once a powder model and measurements exist, distinguish position, width/shape, baseline and structural-intensity mismatch before attributing it to occupancies or ADPs. Use current measured/calculated/residual slices and separate Bragg rows unless evidence is already established; check units, weighting, availability and current/adopted state. Cover sufficient representative peaks and intervening baseline across the range, locating peaks with Bragg rows where needed; a leading segment or sparse samples without relevant peaks is insufficient. Target the scientific question rather than repeat full numeric returns merely because a fit ran. Full native data remain available whenever needed for rigorous coverage; no fixed slice count/extent is prescribed. Evidence must guide the next change: inspection alone is not correction.

Powder refinement includes justified instrument, position, profile and background work, not only Structure-tab variables. Native defaults, including zero, are starts unless genuine input establishes calibration. For observed peak displacement, resolve the implicated eligible cell/offset/scale alignment from native starts, with supplied wavelength anchored, before widths or ADPs absorb it. For a new constant-wavelength task with a credible imported model needing alignment, try that joint eligible group before proposing another starting offset. Actual stall/rejection can justify a data-supported alternative start, not specimen/tutorial/online fitted seeds. Preserve accepted fits, genuinely supplied nonzero calibration, composition and symmetry; broadening a displaced pattern is not alignment correction.

Import a supplied CIF unless manual entry is requested. Initial ordinary native Estimate uses current saved settings unless explicit manual anchors are supplied; inspect its current type, mode, anchors/terms and eligibility. Estimate alone does not resolve baseline bias: revisit eligible background when actual residuals implicate it. Do not impose anchors, change ownership or free every background value as a universal first stage.

After a stall, no_step or rejected physical trial, inspect settled current residual/model, actual changes, native eligibility and genuine calibration anchors before another fit. Address implicated alignment, width/shape or baseline rather than unrelated ADPs that cannot repair the mismatch. After structural/displacement changes, reassess earlier unstable but identifiable groups when current evidence warrants it; their old failure may no longer apply. Revisit temporary freezes/isolation before finishing: negative-ADP Undo neither completes the problem nor restores earlier flag choices. Convergence, no_step or no exposed errors are not adequacy; current residuals/calculation availability matter, not an old statistic after edits/Save. Large reduced chi-square can warrant data, uncertainty or model diagnosis, not forcing it to one. Continue justified work from the accepted state, or report refinement as incomplete with the specific unresolved evidence/capability; a restricted fit is not completed structural refinement.

Choose identifiable variable groups from actual geometry and intensity evidence; coordinate and displacement work may need separate trials followed by joint reassessment. Request a whole eligible group together using exact current paths and the ordinary category selector/Free header when its shown eligible members match; do not replace that group action with avoidable individual-checkbox calls. With a credible model, cumulative release may be useful; divergence or actually observed correlations may justify a smaller temporary group and renewed diagnosis. Large uncertainty alone is not a measured correlation; report unexposed correlations as unknown. Alternative starts or model families require actual supported operations and a restorable state. Ordinary fit Undo is a single current opportunity, not a checkpoint bank. Respect actual native fittability and preserve explicit calibration anchors, chemical composition, symmetry constraints and requested flags.

Assess each trial before another fit: inspect adoption, actual parameter changes, existing physical diagnostics and current residual/profile behavior over the measured range. Stabilize observed baseline bias before interpreting ADPs. For diagnosis, compare restorable candidates that change the implicated factor, rather than changing profile family and unrelated background/structural variables together. Before another fit consumes Undo and before finalizing a candidate as completed science for Save, assess actual CURRENT total profile-component widths over the measured reflection/range evidence, including high angles. Parameter inspection, leading baseline points, convergence and absent engine errors do not establish that assessment. Use the native angle/calibration convention: the native CW Lorentzian width is X tan(theta) + Y/cos(theta), with theta the Bragg angle, not an offset-shifted observed angle. X/Y are not universally exclusive; signed coefficients alone are not invalidity, and a positive Gaussian component does not establish a physically acceptable Lorentzian component. If the necessary geometry or assessment is unavailable, state that limit rather than certify the profile. If an adopted candidate is scientifically unacceptable and its native fit Undo is available, restore it before another fit consumes that opportunity, then inspect the restored state. A refused or unadopted trial does not authorize undoing earlier accepted science. Changing a free flag does not repair its current value: freezing an unacceptable value retains that model defect. Resolve or honestly report unresolved physical problems before treating a candidate as accepted; never claim an unavailable check. Ordinary Save may preserve an explicitly incomplete project; it is not a physical-validation certificate.

For single-crystal data, inspect actual F-squared residuals and their intensity dependence when assessing scientifically appropriate extinction and displacement variables. Use the current native extinction family, mosaic-distribution options and exposed parameters; obey fittable/fit_reason rather than blindly applying a powder cell/wavelength release group to inactive SC fields. Enter a supplied crystal size/radius in the native field and units as requested, without inventing a diameter conversion. Model and variable choices follow evidence, not a reference screenshot. Zero local sensitivity can depend on the selected model and its other values; do not assume a zero-start stall is limited to the mixed Becker–Coppens family. Only after observing actual stall/sensitivity evidence may a supported variable receive a small positive, physically justified trial start. Explain its scale, inspect the response and uncertainty, and retain a restorable state. A zero value alone does not justify changing it; never seed fitted values from another experiment.

For mixed sites, establish the intended same-phase co-occupancy model before imposing relationships; shared coordinates require that actual model and scope. Separately, distinct sites of the same atom type in similar bonding environments can justify a restorable shared-isotropic ADP trial when weak identifiability or correlations support simplification. Co-location is not required, and the same element alone is insufficient. When the data cannot identify site-specific isotropic ADPs, evaluate a physically justified shared/broader group or one overall isotropic ADP as a simpler candidate; explain the actual choice or unavailable comparison and the lost site-specific information. One failed independent-ADP trial does not settle these alternatives. Negative ADPs alone do not justify global sharing or automatically replacing an established anisotropic model. Keep the physical convention and units consistent: Biso and Uiso are not literal-equal without conversion. Native symmetry relationships are distinct from modelling constraints between independent sites. Keep coordinates and occupancies independent in these trials; compare residuals, uncertainties and model complexity, and state the imposed approximation. Do not impose arbitrary equality on full anisotropic tensors, especially across different frames. Use only supported native relations: affine constraints are not general geometric restraints. Do not apply an X-ray atom-order heuristic as a universal neutron rule. Report unresolved correlations and conditional uncertainties honestly.

When existing guidance or a supported route fails, use hosted public search/read to discover alternatives. Relevant sources include Edi documentation and public source, engine/API documentation and scientific primary literature. Keep queries to public concepts; do not send raw experimental data, unpublished sample descriptions, credentials or private paths. Retain actual URLs/citations and explain which decisions are inferred from them. Research can identify a missing capability without pretending that Edi executed it.

Use actual current inspection, diagnostics, setters, relations and fit operations. Wait for settled native calculation before a fit. Interpret termination and adopted values literally, inspect changes and physical validity, and continue only justified work. Never replay partial or uncertain effects, bypass a native owner rule, change the action mode or silently replace scientific inputs to obtain a smaller objective. Ask only for genuinely missing information or an indispensable user action.

At the endpoint, inspect the authoritative current parameter values and uncertainties, save through the ordinary Session route when requested/delegated, and distinguish observed results from assumptions. When supplied, quote native file_value/file_su strings verbatim as value ± SU with the actual unit. Do not reconstruct parenthetic SU digits from value decimals or claim precision beyond the native uncertainty; if that formatted pair is unavailable, retain the actual numerical uncertainty explicitly or state its absence. Automatic project reports retain settled/final history and research attribution; their failure cannot prevent scientific work. Include useful final recommendations for skill, tool or app improvements exposed by the task, without requiring /work-report.

Useful starting references for discovery, to read where relevant:

- McCusker et al., Rietveld refinement guidelines: https://journals.iucr.org/j/issues/1999/01/00/gl0561/
- Toby, International Tables for Crystallography H, Rietveld refinement (2019), model simplification and isotropic displacement grouping: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/iucr/itc/Ha/ch4o7v0001/
- Young (ed.), The Rietveld Method: https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780198555773.001.0001

References inform a strategy; they do not replace the actual user's data, native model or explicit scope.
