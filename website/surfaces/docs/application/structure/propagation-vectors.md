## Use this group

On **Structure → Main**, expand **Propagation vectors**. Edit, add or remove the structure's commensurate k vectors. Each change is checked against the cell and the magnetic space group; a vector they cannot carry, or one already listed, is refused with its reason. The last vector stays.
