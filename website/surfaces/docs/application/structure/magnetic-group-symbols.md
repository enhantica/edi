## Use this group

On **Structure → Extra**, expand **Magnetic group symbols**. For a magnetic structure it shows the symbols that follow
from its magnetic space group, read only: the UNI symbol, the OG number and symbol, and the point group's UNI symbol
and Litvin number. They are saved with the structure as the magnetic CIF dictionary names them.

See [Magnetic structures](../../physics/magnetic.md) for the model.
