# Survey

Tell us how you analyse diffraction data and what you need from EasyDiffraction. It takes about 15 minutes, every
question is optional, and you can send it at any time. If you are at a workshop or another event, choose it under
**Event** so we can see the answers from that event together.

Your answers are anonymous unless you give your e-mail at the end. They go to the EasyDiffraction developers only.
