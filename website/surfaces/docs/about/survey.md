# Survey

Tell us how you analyse diffraction data and what you need from EasyDiffraction. It takes about 10-15 minutes, every
question is optional, and you can send it at any time. If you are at a workshop or another event, choose it under
**Event** so we can see the answers from that event together.

The survey is anonymous: it asks for no name, e-mail or affiliation. Your answers go to the EasyDiffraction developers
only. To tell us who you are, or to get an answer, use [Get in touch](community.md).
