# About

Hi, I'm Andrew Sazonov. I work at the [European Spallation Source (ESS)](https://ess.eu), in the [Data Management & Scientific Computing centre (DMSC)](https://ess.eu/data-management-scientific-computing). I started EasyDiffraction and still lead its development, together with the people who contribute code, ideas and feedback.

Before joining ESS, I worked as an instrument scientist and spent a lot of time doing experiments as a user myself. During my PhD I saw how hard it could be to get started with diffraction data analysis software. Many tools were powerful, but the learning curve was often frustratingly steep. That experience shaped the main idea behind EasyDiffraction from the very beginning: make diffraction analysis approachable, intuitive and easy to use.

EasyDiffraction started in 2019 as a simple mock-up of a modern, easy-to-use graphical interface. Since then it has grown into a much more capable application, with many improvements to both the interface and what it can do. You can explore what it can do today on the [Features](../user-guide/features.md) page.

User experience has always been at the centre of the project. One encouraging milestone was very positive feedback on an intermediate interface design from the company [Design Psychology](https://www.designpsykologi.dk), which confirmed we were moving in the right direction.

EasyDiffraction has also benefited from contributors, testers and users who shared ideas, reported problems, suggested improvements, or contributed code and documentation. Their support has shaped the project.

You are very welcome to take part too: use EasyDiffraction, share feedback, suggest features, report problems, contribute to the code or documentation, or simply tell us about your experience. If you are interested, write to me directly at <span class="ed-email" data-mail="dWUuc3NlQHZvbm96YXMud2VyZG5h"><noscript>andrew.sazonov [at] ess [dot] eu</noscript></span>, or use the [feedback form](community.md), with your name or anonymously. Every message is read and carefully considered.

I'm also happy to collaborate in other ways, from discussing data analysis to testing, usability feedback and ideas for the future. Another great way to help is the [user survey](survey.md). It is anonymous, takes about 10-15 minutes, and helps us understand what users need and what matters most. Your answers really do shape the future of EasyDiffraction.
