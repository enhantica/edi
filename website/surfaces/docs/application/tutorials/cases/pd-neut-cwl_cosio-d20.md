## Scale, 2θ zero and cell

The scale sets the overall height of the pattern, and the zero shift and the three cell lengths put the peaks where they are measured.

Filter the **Linked structures** and **Cell** parameters, one category at a time, using the category filter and the **Instrument** parameters with **calib** in their name using the category filter and the name filter, click the **Free** column header and choose **Free · all**, then press **Fit** in the status bar.

## Peak widths

The profile terms now fit the widths and shapes of the peaks. diffraction-lib's tutorial also fits a peak asymmetry; this case's profile has none.

## Constrain the two Co sites

The correlations show no strong pair for the two Co sites, yet the fit gives them very different displacements, Co1's even negative, which no atom can have: both are Co and scatter alike, so the pattern cannot tell their thermal motions apart. Give each an alias and constrain Co2's to follow Co1's, starting again from 0.5 Å², then fit again; the shared displacement is positive and its s.u. smaller than either had alone.

Filter the **Atomic displacement** parameters with **[Co2]** in their name using the category filter and the name filter, click the **Free** column header and choose **Fixed · all**, then press **Fit** in the status bar.
