The same staged fit as the Python and Command line tutorials, in the app. Its structure and experiment come from EasyDiffraction's collection with **Load ▾ From web…**, and each stage frees its parameters in the **Parameters** table on **Analysis** before the fit.

## Scale, 2θ zero and cell

The scale, the zero shift and the two cell lengths put the peaks in place and at the right height.

Filter the **Linked structures** and **Cell** parameters, one category at a time, using the category filter and the **Instrument** parameters with **offset** in their name using the category filter and the name filter, click the **Free** column header and choose **Free · all**, then press **Fit** in the status bar.
