# Citation

When you publish a result, say which version of EasyDiffraction you used. The app shows it in its About window, and in Python `edi.__version__` holds it. If a release gives a citation or a DOI, use that one.

## Keep your analysis repeatable

Save the project with your result. It holds the data, the structural model and every setting, so you or someone else can open it and continue. A [report](../application/report/index.md) gives a readable summary of the model and the fit; keep it alongside the project.

Also cite the sources of your data and structural models.
