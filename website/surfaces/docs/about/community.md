# Get in touch

Ask a question, share feedback, report a problem or suggest an idea. Messages reach the EasyDiffraction developers, the
same people as the app's Help and feedback, and every one is read. Give your name and e-mail if you would like an answer,
or leave them empty to write anonymously. To write to Andrew Sazonov, who leads the project, see [About](index.md).

## Send a message

When you report a problem, it helps to say:

- what you did, what happened and what you expected;
- whether you use the browser app, the desktop app, Python or the command line, and its version;
- a small project or file that shows the problem, if you can share it. Leave out unpublished or confidential data.

## Take the survey

Have 10-15 minutes? The [survey](survey.md) is anonymous. It asks how you work and what you need, and your answers
shape what we build next.

## Other ways

You can also open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/enhantica/edi/issues). If you would like to work on the code,
its source is in the [edi repository](https://github.com/enhantica/edi).
